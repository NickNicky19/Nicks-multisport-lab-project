# Descriptive daily research scorecards

This definition is registered before the first daily-snapshot game or genuine
grade. It changes reporting, not R0/K0 probabilities, model selection or a frozen
experiment. Historical reconstructions, development records and prospective
daily research remain separate. No PROS004 or DEV comparative aggregate is run.

The population is eligible, genuinely issued daily originals with their active
statistical outcome/grade. Count original forecasts, distinct players and events,
scored appearances, explicit DNP and pending/unresolved evidence separately.
Corrections replace the active reporting view through their append-only links;
original records and previous grades remain intact. DNP is not zero production.
Never pool the two models into an apparent independent sample of twice as many
players. Every score is conditional on appearance, as the original forecast was.

Group numerical summaries by evidence lane, model and category. User filters use
UTC event date and actual issue-to-game lead time: below 90 minutes, 90 through
strictly below 180 minutes, or at least 180 minutes. These are reporting groups,
not a changed issuance policy. Forecast horizon and source age remain distinct.

Point error is forecast mean minus observed count; bias is its average and MAE
is the average absolute error. Report the existing proper CRPS and log loss from
the retained category grades. Any zero-support observation makes aggregate log
loss infinite; show the count rather than dropping failures. Brier applies only
to the registered binary DD/TD outcomes. Inclusive 80% interval coverage is the
fraction of scored observations between the original interval endpoints.

For a discrete calibration diagnostic, fix nominal quantiles 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9.
For each original marginal find its quantile value q and its actual CDF F(q).
Compare mean F(q) with the observed frequency of Y <= q. Do not compare directly
with the nominal quantile: mass at q can make F(q) larger than that nominal value.
Show counts and the discrete-tie interpretation. This descriptive display is not
a calibration certification or a confirmatory test; it makes no promotion,
profitability or ticket-qualification decision.

No observations means unavailable metrics, not zero error or perfect coverage.
Small samples, paired player outcomes, old training history, missing injury and
lineup information, finite-support failures and category-specific uncertainty
remain limitations. The first four-player operating cycle is not league-wide
forecast evaluation. Financial settlement and paper-ticket returns are separate.
