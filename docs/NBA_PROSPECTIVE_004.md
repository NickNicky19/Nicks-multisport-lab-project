## Primary comparisons and uncertainty

The primary family is the **14 per-category paired mean discrete CRPS/RPS
differences K0 minus R0** on exactly the same scored appearances. Negative favors
K0. Do not average raw errors across target units. DD/TD binary CRPS equals Brier;
there is no separate duplicated primary Brier hypothesis.

Retain the 21 registered calendar-date clusters, including valid-empty/failure
dates. Use 20,000 complete-date cluster bootstrap draws, Python `random.Random`
seed `20261021004`. Each draw samples 21 dates with replacement, retains all
their selected subjects and recomputes paired score-difference sums divided by
that draw's paired appearance count. A draw with no paired appearances is marked
undefined and counted, never redrawn; if any such draw occurs, inferential
advancement is unavailable. Forecasts are not refitted inside draws.

Report raw point differences, descriptive two-sided 95% percentile intervals,
and one-sided upper percentile bounds at **`1-0.05/14`**, using linear
interpolation. The latter is a prespecified Bonferroni allocation across all
fourteen primary comparisons, including PA whether or not its result is favorable.
These cluster-bootstrap bounds are approximate, not a finite-sample familywise
guarantee. Complete dates retain within-game/player dependence; dependence across
dates, repeated players and three weeks of possible drift remain limitations.
The sample is the fixed low-numeric-ID roster cohort of first daily games and
does not establish full-roster/full-slate or universal NBA accuracy.

## Category advancement and coverage/tail guards

`ADVANCE_FOR_FURTHER_RESEARCH` means only that a category is a candidate for a
later independently frozen study. It never qualifies a price, ticket, model or
probability. All categories and both coherent laws remain in the results even
when a category fails. No successful marginal may be spliced into another law;
any eventual joint-model promotion must reevaluate affected sums, DD/TD and
dependence under a new experiment.

Every condition below is necessary; no result can substitute for a failed guard:

1. At least **20 distinct scored event-start dates and 80 paired APPEARED
   subjects**, with both-model timely issuance for at least **95% of all frozen
   selected subjects** and verified final APPEARED/DNP outcomes for at least
   **95% of timely paired subjects**. Report both denominators and exact counts;
   do not condition issuance coverage on eventual participation. A missing
   selected event is four failed selected subjects. No permission, identity,
   event-version, clock or receipt-integrity violation is allowed.
2. The category's primary Bonferroni-allocated upper bound is **strictly below
   zero**. A crossing/equal-to-zero bound is INCONCLUSIVE, not evidence of parity.
3. K0 has **zero observed zero-support outcomes** for that category; any zero
   mass makes its unsmoothed aggregate log loss infinite and advancement fails.
   Retain every such row in CRPS, support counts and diagnostics. Do not add a
   probability floor or drop it after seeing the outcome.
4. For K0's central **50%, 80% and 95%** category intervals, the absolute
   difference between observed coverage and mean predicted interval mass is at
   most **0.05**. Use the actual discrete predicted mass, not nominal coverage.
   Its observed rate above the 95% endpoint must exceed the corresponding mean
   predicted tail mass by at most **0.02**. Its observed zero frequency must
   differ from mean predicted zero probability by at most **0.05**. These are
   prespecified finite-sample research guardrails, not calibration certification.
5. Reported-minute 95% coverage under K0 must be within **0.03** of mean predicted
   interval mass and at most **0.01 below R0** observed coverage. This common
   workload guard applies to every category, preventing a favorable count score
   from concealing the DEV003 workload-coverage limitation. Report minute MAE,
   RMSE, CRPS, 50/80/95% widths and coverages at the retained rounded precision.
6. DD and TD additionally require at least **20 observed positives and 20
   negatives** each. BLK requires at least **20 nonzero outcomes**. Otherwise
   their advancement status is INCONCLUSIVE regardless of a favorable mean
   score/bound. Small rare-event counts do not certify rare-event reliability.

Also report full DD/TD probability and event frequencies, finite/infinite log
diagnostics, zeros, support extrema, interval widths and observed/predicted upper
tail rates for every category. Preserve point diagnostics for personal-history
